Walk to earn screen time

Balpum turns your steps into screen time. By default 100 steps is 1 minute, on top of 15 free minutes every day. Minutes you don't spend carry over to the next day, up to 60.

When your balance hits 0, a lock screen covers the apps you picked. It never truly blocks you — “Open anyway” is always there — but that day gets a smudge on your record.

Frequently asked questions

My steps aren't showing up

Balpum reads steps from the Apple Health app. Check that step reading is enabled under iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Balpum. Syncing happens when you open the app, and pulling down on the home screen syncs again right away.

The lock won't turn on

Locking needs separate Screen Time access. You can request it under Lock settings on the home screen. This access is used only to manage your own device; it never supervises anyone else's.

Why can't I see app names in the lock list?

iOS hands your selection to the app in a form it cannot interpret. So Balpum cannot know which apps you picked, and the screen shows only a count. This is a constraint iOS enforces.

What happens when I tap “Open anyway”?

It asks once more, then opens. That day gets a smudge (a brown dot), it counts in your weekly report, and the app locks again after 15 minutes.

If I change the rates, what happens to past days?

They are recalculated too. Your balance is never a stored number — it is computed from your step history every time.

What does the Apple Watch add?

Start a session on your Watch and walking at Zone 2 heart rate (110 bpm by default) earns 1.5×. Your wrist is tapped every 100 steps, and the session is saved as a workout so it fills your Activity rings.

I don't see the Weekly Nag

It stays hidden until you have some history. The single sentence at the top of the card appears only on devices that support Apple Intelligence; the summary below it is identical on every device.

Will my history move to a new device?

No. Balpum has no account, no server, and no backup — your history lives only on the device. Deleting the app deletes it for good. Workouts saved to the Health app remain there.

Requirements

  • iPhone — iOS 18 or later
  • Apple Watch — watchOS 11 or later (optional)
  • The one-line comment in Weekly Nag requires an Apple Intelligence capable device
